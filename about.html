ABOUT ALAPON

More than event planners

ALAPON was founded in 2005 with the vision of taking responsibility for the work that surrounds an important occasion. Instead of leaving clients to chase vendors, timelines and logistics, we share that burden.

Your people behind the celebration

ALAPON was built around a practical idea: important occasions deserve personal attention, not a one-size-fits-all package. We handle the logistics, timelines, and vendors to make your event journey completely effortless.

OUR STORY

Our philosophy is straightforward: manage the event with the care and involvement you would expect from a trusted member of your own family. Professional planning stays at the centre, but personal attention shapes how we work.

WHY THE NAME MATTERS

আলাপন

“আলাপন” means conversation in Bengali. Events create a similar space. People come together, stories are exchanged, relationships are renewed and a room becomes more than a room. That is why every gathering we build is designed to feel as effortless, warm, and natural as a great conversation.
